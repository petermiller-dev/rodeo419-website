§Privacy

Privacy

Short version. This website doesn't track you, doesn't set cookies, and doesn't collect anything you type.

01

What this site collects

Nothing, directly. This website has:

  • No analytics or tracking. No Google Analytics, no advertising pixels, no session recording.
  • No cookies.
  • Nothing you type is sent to this website. The inquiry form on the Contact page doesn't send anything to this site or to any outside service. It opens your own email app with your answers filled in, and nothing is sent until you press Send in that app.
  • No embedded third-party services. No maps, videos, social media widgets, chat tools, or fonts loaded from other companies. Fonts and images are served from this site.

The site uses one small script for the menu, the animations, and the inquiry form. It doesn't store anything in your browser. Everything on the site works with it turned off; without it, the Contact page lists the same questions with a link to email me.

02

Hosting

This preview is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard technical information when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep server logs for security. I don't have access to those logs. You can read GitHub's privacy statement (opens in a new tab).

03

Links to other sites

The work pages link to client website previews. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

04

If you email or call

If you contact me by email or phone, I'll use what you send only to reply and to talk about your project. I won't sell it or add you to a mailing list.

05

Changes

If the site starts doing anything different with information, this page will be updated first. Last updated November 2026.
