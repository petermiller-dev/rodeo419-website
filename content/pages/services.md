## Business strategy and messaging {#messaging}

Label: Messaging
Home: yes
Summary: Before I design anything, I learn how your business earns trust and what customers ask before they buy. That becomes the copy, the pages, and a clear path to an inquiry.

A good website starts with the conversation you have with customers every day. Before I design anything, I want to understand how your business earns trust, what people ask before they buy, and what makes your work worth choosing over the next search result.

We'll talk through who your best customers are, what they're worried about when they find you, and what you'd tell them across the counter. Then I turn that into plain-language copy, service pages that answer real questions, a list of the photos that would prove what you say, and one straightforward path from "just looking" to getting in touch.

::: gets What you get
- A working summary of who you serve, what you offer, and why people choose you
- Page-by-page copy written in your voice, for your review
- A site map showing which pages you need and why
- A photo list: what to shoot, where it goes, and what it should show
:::

::: when When it's useful
- Your current site lists services but doesn't explain why anyone should choose you
- Customers keep asking the same questions your website should be answering
- You're starting fresh and aren't sure what to say
:::

## Custom web design {#web-design}

Label: Web design
Home: yes
Summary: A site shaped around your business: layout, type, imagery, and the phone experience are all decided on purpose, including planning the photography.

Your website shouldn't look like your competitor's with the logo swapped. I design each site around the business it's for. A pediatrician's site should feel calm and trustworthy. A heating and air company's site should put the phone number where a stressed homeowner can reach it with one thumb. A family skincare brand needs room for its story and its products.

I'll explain every decision as we go: why the layout is ordered the way it is, why I chose a typeface, which photos go where, and how a visitor moves from your homepage to contacting you. Most of your visitors will be on a phone, so I design the phone version as carefully as the desktop one.

Photography matters more than almost anything else. I plan every photo slot with you: what it should show, how it'll be cropped on desktop and mobile, and whether you need a photographer or can take it yourself. I can help coordinate the shoot.

::: gets What you get
- A custom design for desktop, tablet, and phone
- A working preview you can open on your phone and share
- A photo plan with sizes, crops, and shot descriptions
- A fast, accessible site with no page-builder bloat
:::

::: when When it's useful
- Your site looks dated or doesn't match the quality of your work
- It's hard to use on a phone
- You're launching a new business or a new service
:::

## Website SEO {#website-seo}

Label: Website SEO
Home: yes
Summary: Useful service and location pages, connected with sensible internal links, on a fast site that search engines can read.

Search engines reward pages that answer what people are looking for. For a local business that usually means one clear page for each service you offer, pages for the areas you actually serve, and helpful articles that answer the questions customers ask before they call.

I plan those pages with you, connect related pages so visitors and search engines can follow the thread (from a problem, to the service that fixes it, to the place you serve), and build the site to load fast and be easy for search engines to crawl. Titles, descriptions, headings, structured data, and a sitemap are handled as part of the build, not added later.

If it makes sense for your business, I can help with ongoing articles and page updates, either writing them with your input or editing what you write.

::: gets What you get
- A page plan for your services and service areas
- Internal links that connect problems, services, and locations
- Technical basics: speed, clean markup, titles, descriptions, sitemap, structured data
- Optional ongoing article and content support
:::

::: when When it's useful
- People can't find you when they search for what you do in your area
- You have one page trying to cover every service
- You're adding services or expanding into new areas
:::

## Google Business Profile {#google-business-profile}

Label: Google Business Profile
Home: yes
Summary: Your listing on Google Search and Maps, set up accurately and kept up: categories, services, service areas, photos, and a simple review routine.

For many local businesses, the Google Business Profile is the first thing a customer sees, before they ever reach your website. It's the listing with your hours, your reviews, your photos, and the "Call" and "Directions" buttons on Google Search and Maps.

I'll help you get it right and keep it that way:

- **Categories and services.** Choosing the primary category that best describes your business, adding secondary categories only where they fit, and listing your services in the words customers use.
- **Accurate business details.** Name, phone, website, hours, and service areas that match your website and what you actually do.
- **Photos.** A plan for the photos that help people choose you: your work, your team, your storefront or vehicle, and what customers will see when they arrive.
- **Verification guidance.** Google decides how and whether a profile is verified. I'll walk you through the steps they ask for and help you prepare.
- **Reviews.** A simple routine for asking happy customers for a review and for responding to every review, good or bad, in a way that helps the next customer decide.

I can't promise rankings, verification, or a number of calls. Google controls those. What I can do is make sure your profile is complete, accurate, consistent with your website, and kept up to date, which is the part you control.

::: gets What you get
- A profile review with a list of what to fix
- Category, service, and service-area recommendations
- A photo plan and an update routine
- A review request and response routine you can actually keep up with
:::

::: when When it's useful
- You don't have a profile yet, or you're not sure who set it up
- Your listing has wrong hours, an old phone number, or missing services
- You get good work from referrals but few reviews online
:::


## Website care and ongoing updates {#care}

Label: Site care
Summary: Hosting oversight, updates, and small changes after launch, so the site stays accurate.

A website isn't finished at launch. Hours change, services change, you hire someone, you finish a project worth showing. A care plan keeps your site accurate without you having to remember how it works.

Care can include keeping an eye on hosting and your domain, making text and photo updates, adding new pages or articles, keeping your Google Business Profile in step with your website, and a short check-in so we can plan what's next.

::: gets What you get
- Hosting and domain oversight
- Text, photo, and page updates
- A periodic check-in on your website and Google profile
:::

::: when When it's useful
- You'd rather run your business than learn a website editor
- Your business changes often: seasonal hours, new services, new work to show
:::
