04About

One owner, working directly with other owners

When you hire Rodeo 419, you work with me. I'm the one who asks the questions, writes the copy, designs the pages, and answers the phone when something needs fixing.

01

How I work with local businesses

I started Rodeo 419 for the kind of business owner who does excellent work and doesn't have time to explain it online. A pediatrician who takes time to listen to parents. A heating and air company that gives honest second opinions. A family making skincare the way their grandmother taught them. They're good at what they do, and their websites weren't saying so.

My job is to close that gap. I spend most of the early time listening, because the best website copy usually comes out of the owner's own mouth. Then I build a site that says those things clearly, looks like it belongs to that business, and makes it easy for the right customer to get in touch.

02

What you can expect from me

  • Plain English. I'll explain what I'm doing and why, without the jargon.
  • Honest answers. If something won't help your business, I'll say so, even if it's something you could pay me for.
  • No promises I can't keep. Nobody can guarantee rankings or a number of calls. I'll tell you what I can control and do it well.
  • Your business, your voice. The site should sound like you on a good day, not like an agency.
  • Real previews. You'll review a working site on your own phone, not a picture of one.

03

The meaning of the name

People ask about the name, so here's the story.

Rodeo is for Fort Worth. This is a city where the Stockyards still matter, where the Stock Show and Rodeo bring everyone together every winter, and where a lot of businesses are still owned by the families who started them. Rodeo stands for the place and for that kind of work ethic: show up, do the job right, take care of people.

419 is for Philippians 4:19.

04

The foundation of the business

I'm Catholic, and my faith is the foundation of how I run this business. I don't put that on the website to sell anything. I put it here because it shapes how I work, and you should know who you're hiring.

In practice, it means I try to treat every client's business the way I'd want mine treated: tell the truth, charge fairly, do careful work even when nobody's checking, and remember that a small business usually supports a family. Philippians 4:19 is a reminder that the work is provided for, so I don't need to oversell it.

You don't have to share my faith to work with me. You can expect me to live up to it.
